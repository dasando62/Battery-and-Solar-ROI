About the Analyser

This analyser helps you estimate the return on investment (ROI) for installing a home solar panel and battery system. It uses your actual energy usage data (or estimates you provide) to simulate performance over time, considering system degradation, changing tariffs, and financing options.

The goal is to provide a detailed, data-driven comparison of different system configurations and electricity providers, helping you make informed decisions when evaluating quotes.
